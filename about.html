What is Diasporic Identity about?

Diasporic Identity navigates how this identity can be confusing, fragmented and the complications that come with them.

It is about my personal experience with my own diaspora and how i understand it. Whether it’s through my understanding of my own country and the movement it comes with moving to place to place.

From my birthplace Al’Bayda Yemen as a safe heaven from war for my mother to where I am actually from Hargeisa, Somalia to my hometown Portland, Maine.

Diaspora

What is a diaspora?

When people who
originate from a country
disperse from the
country to various
locations throughout the
world. While maintaining
the culture
and traditions in the areas
they have immigrated
to.

Usually, diaspora’s exist
due to colonialism, wars
and natural disasters
but the most common
reason due to the
existence of diaspora’s
is due to colonialism
because colonialism is a
direct effect of what
creates war and conflict
within non-white
countries that have
been colonized.

For instance, I am from
a diaspora myself, the
Somali diaspora, but as
the Black/African
diaspora. This website
goes into diaspora,
particularly the Somali
diaspora.

What is considered a home in this
context of a diasporic identity?

Always moving, never
still, nowhere to call a
home, my home is 3,000
miles away; the memory
of what a supposed
“home” is is fragmented
due to this diasporic
identity. 

Disclaimers on Diasporic Identity

This website is purely for navigating the diasporic identity within myself, I am not a spokesperson for the diaspora and country I am from. This is not mean’t as an educational as one person can’t speak for millions of people for a particular country.
